===============================================================================
GRADING LOGIC - CLASSES 9 AND 10 (SSC)
SchoolTrack / Matkhola ML High School
===============================================================================

How a mark becomes a grade, and how grades become a GPA.

This describes what the software actually DOES, not grading rules in general.
Every rule below is in web/src/data/grading.ts and its twin
android/.../data/Grading.kt, pinned by 37 test cases on each side.

Nothing is stored. Grades, totals and GPA are worked out from the marks every
time, so correcting one mark corrects the marksheet, the tabulation, the
progress report and the merit list together. Only publishing an exam freezes
it.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHICH SUBJECTS A STUDENT SITS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A result is only as right as the list of subjects it is built from, so this
comes first. Each subject carries three filters and a student must pass all
three to sit it:

   Class     - the subject's class list must contain the student's class
   Stream    - empty means everyone; otherwise must contain the student's group
   Religion  - empty means everyone; otherwise must contain their religion

A student whose group or religion is BLANK is excluded by nothing. A missing
field means "not known yet", not "does not match", so a half-entered student
still appears on every sheet instead of quietly vanishing.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FROM PARTS TO A SUBJECT TOTAL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Most papers are marked in PARTS, and each part has its own maximum AND its own
pass mark:

   Shape                          Pass marks     Subjects
   ----------------------------   ------------   -----------------------------
   CQ 70 + MCQ 30                 23 and 10      Bangla, Maths, Religion, BGS,
                                                 General Science, Civics,
                                                 History, Geography,
                                                 Accounting, Finance,
                                                 Business Entrepreneurship
   CQ 50 + MCQ 25 + Practical 25  17, 8 and 8    Physics, Chemistry, Biology,
                                                 Higher Maths, Agriculture
   CQ 25 + MCQ 25  (out of 50)    8 and 8        ICT
   Written 100                    0              English 1st, English 2nd

The subject total is the sum of its parts.

ONE PART STILL BLANK MEANS NO TOTAL - not a zero. A subject whose practical
has not been marked yet has no total, no grade and no GPA, and every sheet
shows a dash. A student is never failed for work a teacher has not finished
entering.

The screen can still show what HAS arrived, which matters most for English:
1st and 2nd Paper are one subject out of 200, so until the second paper is in
the subject has no mark at all. The sheet shows the part that has been keyed
in, so the teacher can see their work went somewhere.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. A FAILED PART FAILS THE SUBJECT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the rule people are surprised by, and the reason parts are stored at
all rather than added up and forgotten:

      60 in CQ and 8 in MCQ is 68 out of 100 - AND STILL F,
      because the MCQ pass mark is 10.

The order the software applies, and the order matters:

   1. Absent                        -> F, 0.00
   2. Any part not yet marked       -> no grade, a dash. NOT a fail.
   3. Any part below ITS OWN pass   -> F, 0.00, however high the total.
                                       The failed part is named, so the
                                       marksheet can say which one.
   4. Otherwise                     -> the percentage scale in section 4.

English passes at 0 per part, so the English papers can never fail on the part
rule - only on the percentage.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE LETTER SCALE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Applied to the subject's PERCENTAGE of its own full marks:

   From      Letter    Point
   -------   ------    -----
   80%       A+        5.00
   70%       A         4.00
   60%       A-        3.50
   50%       B         3.00
   40%       C         2.00
   33%       D         1.00
   below 33% F         0.00

33% is the pass mark. ICT is out of 50, so 33% of it is 16.5 - the scale is
always a percentage of THAT paper's full marks, never a fixed number of marks.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. COMBINED PAPERS - BANGLA AND ENGLISH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bangla 1st and 2nd Paper are ONE subject out of 200. So are the two English
papers. Grading them separately gives a different GPA for the same marks - two
A- grades where the school's own sheet shows a single A.

So the papers are merged into one unit BEFORE anything is graded:

   - The unit takes the place of the FIRST of its papers, so Bangla stays
     where Bangla was on the sheet instead of dropping to the bottom.
   - Full marks add up: 100 + 100 = 200. So do the totals.
   - If EITHER paper is unmarked the unit has no total, which is why a
     half-entered English shows a dash rather than a bad grade.
   - Each part keeps its paper's name in front of it - "BANGLA 1ST PAPER MCQ",
     not "MCQ". A student told they failed "MCQ" would not know which paper.
   - The unit is named from the common part of its papers: "BANGLA".

A combine group with only ONE paper in it is left alone. ICT is tagged with a
combine group but is a single paper, so it is graded on its own. Harmless, but
worth knowing before someone goes looking for a bug.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE GPA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Worked out over the graded UNITS - after combining, and only the ones that
student sits.

            sum of compulsory points  +  optional bonus
   GPA  =   -------------------------------------------      capped at 5.00
                  number of compulsory subjects

   - THE OPTIONAL (4th) SUBJECT ONLY EVER HELPS. Points ABOVE 2.00 are added
     to the top of the fraction. It is never counted in the divisor, and
     failing it costs nothing.
        optional A+ (5.00) adds 3.00
        optional A  (4.00) adds 2.00
        optional C  (2.00) adds 0.00
        optional F or absent adds 0.00 and cannot fail the student
   - The answer is rounded to two decimals, and the overall letter is read
     from the rounded number.

THE OVERALL LETTER, FROM THE GPA

   GPA            Letter
   ------------   ------
   5.00           A+
   4.00 - 4.99    A
   3.50 - 3.99    A-
   3.00 - 3.49    B
   2.00 - 2.99    C
   1.00 - 1.99    D
   below 1.00     F

This is read from the GPA, not from any single subject. A+ needs the full
5.00, which is why the cap at 5.00 matters.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. FAILED, INCOMPLETE, ABSENT - THREE DIFFERENT THINGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   INCOMPLETE   any compulsory subject has no total and the student was not
                marked absent
                -> GPA blank, letter dash, no position in the merit list,
                   nothing published

   FAILED       any compulsory subject grades F
                -> GPA 0.00, letter F, and the failed subjects are named

   ABSENT       marked absent in a compulsory subject
                -> that subject is F, so the exam is failed

An OPTIONAL subject may be missing without making the result incomplete. A
student takes one 4th subject, not every optional one the class offers.

An incomplete result is never published and never ranked - it is left out of
the merit order rather than placed last, because "not finished" is not a
position.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TOTAL MARKS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The printed total follows EXACTLY the same test as the GPA - withheld while
any compulsory subject is unmarked.

   - An optional subject with no mark is left out of BOTH sides. Counting its
     100 full marks against a student who never sat it would read as a zero.
   - An optional subject that WAS sat counts on both sides, so the total can
     be larger than the compulsory out-of.
   - An absent subject counts 0 obtained, and its full marks in the out-of.

This rule exists because the other way round was built first and found in use:
a sheet showed "GPA 4.33" beside "Total -", which reads as a mistake in the
arithmetic even though both numbers were right by their own rules.

9. WORKED EXAMPLES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All figures below are the software's own output, not arithmetic done by hand.

9.1  A SCIENCE STUDENT, EVERYTHING PASSED

   Subject                        Marks        %     Letter   Point
   ----------------------------   ----------   ---   ------   -----
   Bangla (1st + 2nd)             150 / 200    75    A        4.00
   English (1st + 2nd)            130 / 200    65    A-       3.50
   Mathematics                     82 / 100    82    A+       5.00
   ICT                             40 /  50    80    A+       5.00
   Islam & Moral Education         71 / 100    71    A        4.00
   Bangladesh & Global Studies     66 / 100    66    A-       3.50
   Physics                         60 / 100    60    A-       3.50
   Chemistry                       55 / 100    55    B        3.00
   Biology                         72 / 100    72    A        4.00
   Higher Maths (OPTIONAL)         78 / 100    78    A        4.00

   Compulsory points: 4.00 + 3.50 + 5.00 + 5.00 + 4.00 + 3.50 + 3.50 + 3.00
                      + 4.00  =  35.50,  over 9 compulsory subjects
   Optional bonus   : 4.00 - 2.00 = 2.00

   GPA   = (35.50 + 2.00) / 9 = 4.17   ->   A
   TOTAL = 804 out of 1150

   The optional subject was sat, so its 78 marks AND its 100 full marks count
   on both sides of the total.

   Had this student taken no 4th subject, the same compulsory marks would read
   726 out of 1050, and the GPA would be 35.50 / 9 = 3.94 (A-).

   3.94 -> 4.17 is what the optional subject is worth.


9.2  THE SAME STUDENT, THREE MARKS LOWER IN ONE PLACE

   Chemistry: CQ 40, MCQ 7, Practical 8. Total 55 - exactly as above.
   But the MCQ pass mark is 8.

   CHEMISTRY IS F.
   GPA 0.00, letter F, and Chemistry named as the reason.

   Every other grade still prints on the marksheet exactly as in 9.1, so the
   student can see that one part of one paper did it.


9.3  A HUMANITIES STUDENT, ONE SUBJECT NOT YET ENTERED

   Geography has CQ 55 keyed in and the MCQ box still empty.

   Geography has no total, so the whole result is INCOMPLETE:
      GPA   : blank
      Total : blank
      Merit : not ranked
      Nothing is published.

   Entering the missing MCQ mark completes it. Nothing else has to be redone.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   - No grace marks, and no rounding 32 up to 33. A pass mark is a threshold,
     not a target.
   - No "golden A+" as a separate award. It is visible from the subject
     grades and plays no part in the GPA.
   - No weighting. Every compulsory subject counts once, whatever its full
     marks: ICT out of 50 carries the same weight as Physics out of 100,
     which is how the SSC scale works.
   - No stored grades. Nothing is frozen except by publishing an exam.

11. WHERE IT LIVES IN THE CODE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                      android/.../data/Grading.kt

                      android/.../data/GradingTest.kt
   Who sits what      sitsSubject / excludedBy, in exams.ts
   Tabulation, merit  sheetFor / tabulate, in exams.ts

                      (parts, pass marks, streams, optional, full marks)

The two versions must agree exactly - a student will be shown a GPA by
whichever app is to hand. The same test cases run against both, so changing
one without the other fails the build.
